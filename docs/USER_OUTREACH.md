# CredShield — User Testing Outreach

## About CredShield

CredShield is a privacy-focused creditworthiness verification dApp built on Midnight.

It allows users to prove that they meet creditworthiness requirements without publicly revealing their exact credit score, DTI, or bank balance.

## Live Demo

https://cred-shield.vercel.app

## What We Need

We are looking for users to test the CredShield Preprod demo and share quick feedback about their experience.

### Testing Steps

1. Open the CredShield demo.
2. Connect your 1AM Wallet on Midnight Preprod.
3. Enter test financial values.
4. Generate the private ZK proof.
5. Approve the transaction in 1AM Wallet.
6. Confirm that the verification succeeds.
7. Share your Midnight Preprod wallet address.
8. Share any feedback or issues you noticed.
